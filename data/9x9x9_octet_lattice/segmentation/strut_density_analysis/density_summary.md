## Interpretation

The registered JSON defines where ideal struts should exist but does not contain observed defects. Raw CT, mask occupancy, equivalent thickness, and continuity supply the measured evidence.
Classification prevalence is inferred from the current score distribution and continuity; it is not forced to match literature.
